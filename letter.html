My love,

How it started

I still remember how something so simple slowly became something so important to me. I never knew that one person could quietly become such a beautiful part of my everyday life.

What you mean to me

You are more than someone I love. You are someone who makes ordinary days feel warmer, difficult days feel lighter, and beautiful moments feel even more beautiful simply because I get to share them with you.

Things I've never said enough

Thank you for being you. Thank you for the little things you probably don't even realize I notice. Thank you for the moments that became memories, and for all the times your presence made my heart feel at home.

The way I see you

I wish, just once, you could see yourself through my eyes. You would see someone worth believing in, someone worth being proud of, someone with a heart more beautiful than he probably realizes. I see your strength, but I also see the parts of you that get tired, uncertain, or afraid. And none of those parts make me love you less. They make you human. They make you real. And they make me want to stand beside you even more.

What I promise

I promise to keep choosing you, especially in the ordinary moments. Not only when everything feels beautiful, but when life becomes complicated, quiet, frustrating, or uncertain. I promise to keep appreciating the person you are and to keep learning the person you are becoming. I promise to celebrate your victories, support your dreams, listen to your worries, and remind you of your worth whenever you forget it yourself.

My prayer for you

On your birthday, one of my deepest prayers is that you are always protected, guided, and surrounded by goodness. May every door that is meant for you open at the right time. May the things that are not meant for you leave without taking away your peace. May your heart remain soft, your dreams remain alive, and your path be filled with blessings. And wherever life takes you, I pray you always have a reason to believe that beautiful things are still waiting for you.Allah bless you with everything

On your birthday

Today is your birthday, but somehow I feel like I am the one who has received the gift. Because the world gave me you. And no matter how many birthdays come and go, I hope you always remember that somewhere in this world there is a heart that is endlessly grateful that you exist. I hope this little letter reminds you of how loved you are, how deeply you are appreciated, and how much more beautiful life feels simply because you are a part of mine.

Yours, always.
